{% extends "page_view.html" %} {% comment %} The app shell, its sidebar and its header, each drawn as a diagram of its regions with a table of the blocks that fill them, then how the page sits in the shell. {% endcomment %} {% block page.content %} Every page that extends mvp/base.html is drawn inside the app shell. The shell has five regions, and each one is a block a template can replace. The sidebar and the header have regions of their own, each below. One layout code in MVP_CONFIG["layout"]["view"] arranges them. Every code, and what each letter does, is in django-mvp-layout's documentation.

The page

The page is the content block, in the middle of the shell. It scrolls with the window, under the header, while the header and the sidebars stay in place. Opening or closing a sidebar beside it narrows or widens the page. Below a sidebar's breakpoint the sidebar is a drawer over the page instead, and the page keeps its width. Its title and its side panel are on Page layout.
