Quick tour
==========

This page walks through the three screens you will use most.

The product list
----------------

Every product you stock appears in the product list. Search by name or SKU, and sort
by any column heading.

.. image:: ../_static/icon.svg
   :alt: The Acme Inventory logo
   :width: 96

The product page
----------------

Open a product to see its stock level, its supplier and the last ten movements.

.. tip::

   Press :kbd:`/` anywhere to jump to the search box.

Reorder alerts
--------------

When stock falls below the reorder point, the product is flagged on the dashboard.
See :doc:`../guides/products` for how to set the reorder point.
