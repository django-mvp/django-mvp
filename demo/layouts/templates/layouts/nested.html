Each example below is a <c-layout container>: a whole layout, with its own header, sidebars and footer, drawn inside a card or a dialog. It measures its breakpoints against its own width, never the window's, so narrow the window and each one turns its sidebars into drawers when it runs out of room. Its toggles reach its own sidebars and nothing else.
